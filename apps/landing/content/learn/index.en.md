A series of practical guides to ear training — what it is, how each skill works, and how to practice it. Every guide is written to be used, not just read: each one ends in concrete exercises you can do immediately in the browser.

If you're new here, start with [What Is Ear Training?](/learn/what-is-ear-training) and work down the list in order — each guide builds on the one before it. If you already have the basics, jump straight to the skill you're working on; every guide stands alone.

All the drills referenced in these guides run free at [Oh Your Ear](https://app.ohyourear.com) — no account needed to start, and signing in saves your progress and collects your mistakes for review.
