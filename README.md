Ear training in a browser tab — **single notes, intervals, chords, melodies and rhythms**.
Start without an account; make one later if you want the progress kept.

### The five drills

**Single Note** — one pitch, name it<br />
**Interval** — two pitches, name the distance<br />
**Chord** — a stack of notes, name its quality<br />
**Melody** — a short phrase, pick the one you heard<br />
**Rhythm** — a figure, pick the pattern you heard

Each drill has its own settings — register, how many choices, how the phrase is played — so it goes from three options to name-it-cold.
Practise them three ways: endless random questions, a daily goal, or a graded set that walks from easy to hard.

### Something to play with before you sign up

The landing page has a piano roll you can edit and hear: drag the blocks, double-click to write or delete a note, hit play and watch the spectrum.

### Practise first, sign in later

**As a guest**, every drill is open and scores last for the session.
**With an account**, you get study plans, progress statistics, a notebook of the ones you keep getting wrong, streaks and achievements.
